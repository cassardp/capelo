## Description

**Capelo is a fast, addictive word game. Swipe across a letter grid to form words and chain explosive combos.**

Find words on a 7×7 grid by dragging your finger from letter to letter in all 8 directions. The longer the word, the higher the score. Words of 4+ letters spawn bombs that trigger spectacular chain reactions and multiply your points. Used tiles disappear and new letters drop in — the grid never runs out, so the only limit is the clock.

**How to play**
- Swipe across adjacent letters to form a word (3 letters minimum)
- Release to submit — valid words earn points and add time
- Words of 4+ letters spawn bombs that blast surrounding tiles
- Explosions chain together for massive bonus points
- New tiles fall in to replace every cleared letter — the grid is endless
- The clock is ticking: each word adds time, longer words add more

**4 languages**
Play in English, French, Spanish, or Portuguese. Language is detected automatically, or switch it in settings. Each language has its own dictionary and tailored letter distribution.

**Global leaderboard**
Compare your best score with players worldwide on the All Stars leaderboard. Customize your profile with a username and a link to your project or website.

**Built-in definitions**
After each game, review every word you found. Tap any word to look up its definition straight from Wiktionary.

**Minimalist and distraction-free**
No account required. Just you and the letters.

# Capelo Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I play Capelo?

Swipe across adjacent letters on a 7×7 grid to form words (3 letters minimum). Valid words earn points and add time. Words of 4+ letters spawn bombs that chain-explode nearby tiles for bonus points. New letters drop in to replace cleared tiles — the grid never runs out.

### How does the leaderboard work?

After a game, you can submit your score with a display name to compete on the global All Stars leaderboard. Submitting your score is entirely optional.

### Is an internet connection required?

No. You can play Capelo offline. An internet connection is only needed to view or submit scores to the leaderboard, and to look up word definitions.

### What languages are supported?

Capelo supports English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese. The language is detected automatically from your device settings, or you can change it manually.

### Where is my data stored?

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Leaderboard scores are stored securely on our servers.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove all local data from your device.
